HealthExporter: what to test in this build

- Choose Data has a new layout. Each category (Weight, Steps, Blood Glucose,
  Vitals and each lab panel) is a tile. Tap a tile to turn it on or off. The
  Vitals and lab tiles open a list where you pick single items.
- Exports can be stopped. While "Preparing export..." shows, tap Cancel. The
  screen returns to your selection with no error.
- A partial export says so. If some metrics fail to load, the export goes on
  with the others, and an alert names each metric that failed.
- On iOS 27 you can give Health access that starts at a date. When that date
  is after the start of your export, an alert says when the data for each
  metric starts.
- The disclaimer at the top of the CSV no longer splits into columns when you
  open the file in a spreadsheet.

Please check that the file you export opens in Numbers or Excel, and that the
numbers match the Health app.
